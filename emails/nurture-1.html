Hi there,

SWIM, Savvy Wealth Investment Management, is Savvy’s in-house investment management offering. It brings CIO-designed, tax-aware portfolios and ongoing management to your clients. With a well-rounded range of investment solutions, you can manage all of your clients’ accounts in one place.

You choose which clients to enroll, and when. Your clients stay your clients while we handle the portfolio work behind the scenes.

Time back for what matters
The SWIM team handles day-to-day investment management for the clients you enroll. That gives you hours back for planning conversations, new relationships, and growing your practice.
Investment strategies that fit your clients’ goals
Your clients get access to investment strategies matched to their financial goals, designed by our CIO and managed on an ongoing basis. You deliver quality financial tools without the time that goes into building them yourself.
One place, full picture
All of your clients’ accounts stay in one place, so you can see and manage the whole practice together. You stay in the driver’s seat of every client relationship.
