Hi there,

Advisors tell us the same few things come up when they think about SWIM. Here are straight answers.

Will I get more time with my clients?
Yes. The SWIM team takes over the back-office work of investment management, like research, rebalancing, and trading, so you have more time for client conversations. You still choose which clients to enroll and when, and your clients stay your clients.
What about taxes when I move a client?
SWIM portfolios are tax-aware. Moving a client into a new portfolio can have tax implications, so before you decide, the SWIM team can prepare a transition analysis that shows what a move would involve.
Request a transition analysis
Can I still use my own approach for some clients?
Yes. You decide client by client. SWIM sits alongside how you manage other clients today, and advisor custom models are available when a client needs a more tailored approach.
